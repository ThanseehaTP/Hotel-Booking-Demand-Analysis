# Hotel-Booking-Demand-Analysis
This project analyzes a hotel booking dataset containing reservation, customer, pricing, and cancellation information. Using data cleaning, visualization, and exploratory data analysis techniques, the study identifies booking trends, customer behavior, and factors influencing cancellations to generate valuable business insight for hotel management.
The hotel industry generates large volumes of booking data that can provide valuable insights into customer behavior and reservation patterns. Analyzing hotel booking records helps identify factors influencing cancellations, booking demand, customer preferences, and revenue generation. This project uses exploratory data analysis and visualization techniques to uncover meaningful trends and support data-driven decision-making in hotel management.

OBJECTIVES 
1.To analyze hotel booking patterns and customer reservation behavior.
2.To identify the key factors influencing booking cancellations, such as lead time, deposit type, and customer type.
3.To examine seasonal and monthly trends in hotel bookings using arrival date information.
4.To evaluate the impact of market segments and distribution channels on hotel reservations.
5.To explore customer preferences and booking characteristics through room types, special requests, and length of stay analysis.
6.To identify trends and relationships among different booking-related variables using data visualization techniques in python.
